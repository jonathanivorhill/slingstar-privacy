# Slingstar — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

Slingstar is an arcade game developed by Jonathan Hill ("we"). This policy
explains what data the app handles.

## Data we collect

**None.** We operate no servers and hold no accounts. There is nothing to sign
up for, Slingstar contains no advertising and no analytics, and we never
receive your data. Your progress — scores, coins, skins and settings — is
stored on your device.

One feature does send data off your device:

- **Leaderboards.** If you use the leaderboard, your score and your public
  player name are sent to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS) so
  players can be ranked against one another. These names come from your Google
  or Apple account, not from us, and whether other players can see you is a
  privacy setting in that account rather than in this game.

That is handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We never
see it, and we store nothing about you anywhere.

## Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by the Google Play Store or the Apple App
Store. We never receive or store your payment details.

## Advertising and tracking

Slingstar shows no ads. It does not use an advertising ID, does not track you
across other companies' apps or websites, and does not share data with
advertisers or data brokers.

## Children

Slingstar is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
