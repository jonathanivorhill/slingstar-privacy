# Slingstar — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 25 July 2026_

Slingstar is an arcade game developed by Jonathan Hill ("we"). This policy
explains what data the app handles.

## Data we collect

Slingstar itself collects **no personal data**. Your scores, coins, and
settings are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us.

## Advertising

Slingstar shows ads served by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and process
data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and coarse device
information to serve and measure ads, as described in
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization
in your device's Google settings.

## Children

Slingstar is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
